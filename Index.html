Welcome to Tacos Locos!

Established in 2015, Tacos Locos is a Mexican street food business based in Cape Town. We serve freshly prepared tacos, burritos, samosas and wings, all made daily with quality ingredients and authentic spices. Whether grabbing a quick bite or ordering for a celebration, we bring Mexican flavour to every dish.
